|-----|---------|------------------------|-----|
| Banking affiliate | **Mercury** incorporator/affiliate | Relay Partner (until exclusivity cleared) | Incorporator track; no published exclusivity; CPA negotiable. Relay product is strong for practices but Partner terms (Feb 2026) include competitor exclusivity. |

**Attorney PLLCs:** neither Mercury nor Relay is IOLTA. Recommend operating account + separate NY-bar-approved trust bank.

## Banking — Relay vs Mercury

| Dimension | Relay | Mercury |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Program fit | Partner Program lists formation companies (Aug 2026 help) | **Incorporator & affiliate partners** — clearest fit |
| Apply | [relayfi.com/advisor-partner-program](https://relayfi.com/advisor-partner-program/) | [mercury.com/partnerships](https://mercury.com/partnerships) · partnerships@mercury.com |
| Published payout | **$50–$150** when client funds **≥$1,000 in first 30 days**; **+$100–$200** if **$10k avg balance over 90 days** (tiered). Terms **Feb 1, 2026** | **Not published.** Partner portal only. Consumer referral **$250** needs **$10k in 90 days** — poor proxy |
| Trigger | Approved + funded ($1k / $10k tiers) | Custom (open + balance and/or spend) |
| Payment | Monthly; **into a Relay checking account only** | 1st business day; **US partners need a Mercury account** |
| Product | Multi-account buckets, checks, bookkeeper access — better *practice operating* product | Digital-first; mailed checks; checkbooks rolling out 2026 |
| IOLTA | No | No |
| Healthcare/legal | Operating OK; **card payment requests blocked** for physician/dental/legal NAICS | Law firms OK for operating; no equivalent NAICS card block found |
| Partner risk | **Exclusivity** — cannot refer similar banking products while on Partner | Clawbacks for misleading/bad-faith; no exclusivity found in Apr 2025 referral partner agreement |

**When to mention Relay anyway (product-led, not signed Partner):** client needs multi-account cash management, physical checks, or bookkeeper collaboration — especially non-attorney healthcare. Confirm exclusivity before any paid Relay partnership if Mercury is live.

**Negotiate Mercury for:** CPA on **account approval + first deposit** (target $75–100), not a $10k balance bar.

**EIN-email CTA (draft):** “Open a free business checking account for your new PLLC — apply in ~10 minutes with your Articles and EIN.”  
**Disclosure:** “NYPLLC may earn a commission if you open an account through our link. This does not affect your account terms.” Name Mercury as fintech (Choice/Column N.A., Members FDIC).  
**Attorney addendum:** Mercury/Relay = operating account only; client funds need a separate IOLTA.

## Payroll — Gusto vs ADP

| Dimension | Gusto Impact Affiliate | ADP PartnerStack | ADP Accountant Revenue Share |
|-----------|------------------------|------------------|------------------------------|
| NYPLLC eligible? | ✅ [gusto.com/affiliates](https://gusto.com/affiliates) → Impact | ✅ generic referral | ❌ accounting/bookkeeping firm + 3-client gate |
| Payout | **$200+ per sale** (official); aggregators cite $100–$400 | **$500** (1–4) / **$750** (5–9) / **$1,000** (10+) — agreement **Jun 9, 2026** | Up to 75% of invoice for 12 months — accountant only |
| Trigger | Signup + **first paid payroll** + active at payout | **Converted Client** = **3 payroll runs in 45 days** without gaps | Onboarded + ongoing billing |
| Cookie | **120 days** | ~30 days (verify in dashboard) | PIC / accountant portal |
| S Corp / owner-only | Strong — Reasonable Salary Calculator, owner-as-W-2 | Heavier onboarding; 3-run bar is the problem | N/A |
| Client cost (solo, ~Mar 2026) | ~$55/mo Simple | RUN typically $79+/mo + PEPM | Varies |

**Gusto Pro is the wrong program** (accountant dashboard). Use Impact.

**“20 minutes”:** wizard only. First payroll still needs NY SUI (can be 4–6 weeks), withholding, and a defensible reasonable salary. Honest copy: hours of client effort over days–weeks, not one session.

**S-Corp-Docs-Faxed CTA (draft):** “Set up S Corp owner payroll and run your first W-2 paycheck — Gusto estimates a reasonable salary and handles federal/NY withholding.” Lead product-page copy with **reasonable-salary compliance**, not “save on payroll.”  
**Disclosure:** “NYPLLC may receive compensation if you subscribe through our link.”  
**Do not** bid Gusto trademark terms in paid search (program prohibition).

**ADP only if:** PartnerStack confirms a lower retention bar in writing, or referrals are multi-employee practices already committed to 2+ cycles inside 45 days.
