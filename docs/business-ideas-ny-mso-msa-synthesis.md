## Product

An **up-front MSO package** the customer buys from NYPLLC. After a short pilot, this is a first-purchase offer (not a post-formation add-on). The package is:

1. **Practice entity** — New York **PLLC or PC**, formed by us end-to-end (filing, publication, Certificate of Publication / equivalent). Licensed owners only.
2. **MSO entity** — New York **ordinary LLC** (never a PC or PLLC), formed by us end-to-end the same way, including LLC publication and filing fees. Admin company for the non-clinical work on Schedule A.
3. **MSA referral** — we hand the customer and the fill-in template to **Jonah Brodsky**, who reviews and/or revises the management services agreement and bills the customer **on his end**. We do not sell legal advice or include his fee in our formation price.

Same brand promise as $885 formation: a **fixed, honest packet** — not a PE-style friendly-PC stack, not “we’ll split collections.”

## Bottom line

> **NYPLLC sells two-entity formation (Practice PLLC/PC + admin LLC), all-in. Jonah Brodsky reviews/revises the MSA and charges separately.**

Do **not** market as “we drafted your MSA,” “this fee is FMV,” or “the spouse can own the PLLC.” The customer is buying **structure**: a licensed professional entity plus a non-licensed admin company, with counsel on the contract between them.

Generic “form an LLC in New York” SEO still belongs to CheapNewYorkLLC. This SKU is the **MSO package**, not a competing plain-LLC landing page.

## Best initial customer

Same book as $885 formation — **NY behavioral health launching or professionalizing private practice** (LCSW, LMHC, and adjacent Title VIII):

- Licensed owner needs the PLLC or PC; unlicensed spouse/partner will run books, website, calendar through an ordinary LLC.
- Pain: “Jordan cannot be a member of the PLLC, but we still want them paid for admin.”
- Deal is two humans, two entities, one household, no investors, no practice sale.

Defer: PE roll-ups, DSOs, practice purchases / earnouts, MSO-as-landlord, clinician leasing, anyone who wants a % of collections.

## Operating rules

- **Inter-entity fee:** flat dollars only. 8 NYCRR **29.1(b)(4)** (Title VIII, including social work / MHC) bars pay for space/staff/equipment that is a % of income or receipts. Courts will not enforce illegal splits (*Advanced Dental of Ardsley, PLLC v. Brown*, 2d Dept 2024; *Linchitz Practice Mgt. v Daat Med. Mgt.*, 2018).
- **Ownership:** unlicensed spouse **cannot** own/manage the Practice (LLCL §§ 1204, 1207; BCL §§ 1507, 1508).
- **HIPAA:** MSO that sees PHI (books/AR, calendars with names) is a **BA** even in the same household → Path B + BAA. Workforce of Practice ≠ BA.
- **Related-party:** LLCL § 411 disclosure; **two** signature blocks.
- **Venue:** named **county** (CPLR 501).
- **FIFA (GBL Art. 44-A):** one-person MSO ≥ $800 needs invoice deadline + pay date in writing — the MSA is intended to be that writing.
- **Unit tokens:** street2 is the client’s exact unit (`#2` stays `#2`).
- **Never name Spiffy** (or other internal vendors) in client-facing MSO copy.
