# Customer interview script (P1-07)

**Goal:** ~10 recent buyers + ~10 reachable abandoners. Record actual N if fewer respond.

## Buyers

1. What triggered you to start checkout this week?
2. What almost stopped you from paying?
3. What alternatives did you consider?
4. What made you trust us with a NY professional filing?

## Abandoners

1. Were you ready to form now, or researching for later?
2. Was price, structure/eligibility, missing documents, trust, timeline, or payment friction the main blocker?
3. Anything confusing on the order page or checkout?
4. Would a payment plan or a short call have helped?

Log verbatim phrases before categorizing. Rank top three obstacles by frequency × commercial impact.
