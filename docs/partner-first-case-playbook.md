# Partner first-case playbook (P2-04)

1. Confirm **referral vs wholesale** and who pays the client.
2. Confirm scope, entity type, profession, price tier, referral payout, legal boundary.
3. Provision `?c=` code; verify `Order.b2bPartnerId` on intake (not cookie alone).
4. Collect authorization via existing secure intake.
5. Tell partner: next step, client contact owner, first status update timing (**≤1 business day** response to partner inquiries once committed).
6. Milestone updates: customer wait vs NYSED wait vs internal work.
7. Log staff minutes and rework; compute contribution vs wholesale assumption.
8. Payout only at agreed milestone; reconcile refunds before marking earned.
9. After first milestone: ask for next case + one improvement to the handoff.
