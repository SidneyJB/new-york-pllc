## MSO-ready constraints (in scope at $1,285)

- Shareholders, directors, and officers = **licensees only** in the profession the PC is authorized to practice.
- Certificate purpose = **that profession only**.
- **Single class** of common stock; default **200 no-par** shares.
- **No** management company, Delaware C Corp, spouse, investor, or other unlicensed person on the PC cap table or as officer/director.
- Handoff packet names the **existing PC** after DOS (same timing rule as MSO PLLC intro: counsel needs filed entity name).
