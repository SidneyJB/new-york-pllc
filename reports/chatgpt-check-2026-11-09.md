# ChatGPT channel check — Nov 9 2026

**Why:** ChatGPT sent 3 orders in the week of Nov 2 and 14 since July (CRM `chatgpt.com` referrer). Nobody manages it. This was a one-hour, report-only check approved by Sid on Nov 9.

**Method:** chatgpt.com, logged out, one buyer-style prompt: *"I'm a licensed mental health counselor in New York. What's the best service to form a PLLC in New York, and how much does it cost including publication?"*

## What it answered

- **Top recommendation: "NYPLLC.com"**, shown as a card: **$885, publication included, advertised flat rate**. It linked our LMHC profession page.
- It listed our package accurately:
  - NYSED pre-approval and name review
  - Articles of Organization and the DOS fee
  - EIN and operating agreement
  - First year of registered agent service
  - Six-week publication, affidavits and Certificate of Publication
- It said general services (it named ZenBusiness) don't handle PLLCs.
- The only alternative it named was **Thomas Law Firm PLLC at $2,500, publication extra**.
- It told the user to "confirm the $885 quote includes every government filing, publication charge, and any county-specific surcharge", then asked which county.
- It cited NYSED, NY DOS and our pages.

## Read

- **Position:** we're ChatGPT's default answer for this query, which fits the order trend. No action is needed to "get in".
- **Risk 1 — county surcharge.** ChatGPT invites a county question. Our site deliberately publishes in Rockland and doesn't advertise Manhattan/Brooklyn pricing. Make sure `/ny-pllc-cost` and the FAQ say plainly that the $885 includes publication with no county surcharge for a standard order. Otherwise ChatGPT keeps telling buyers to "confirm".
- **Risk 2 — fragility.** This is one prompt, logged out, on one day. Answers vary by wording and model, and we don't control them.

## Suggestions (Sid decides; nothing changed)

1. Re-run the same prompt once a month, with profession variants (physician, dentist, PT, foreign PLLC), and note the top answer here.
2. Check that the cost FAQ answers the county question in one sentence. That's copy only, after the Dec 1 freeze, unless Sid wants it sooner.
