# Credentialing brand + domain shortlist (Nov 18 2026)

For the standalone credentialing business (decision log, Nov 18: "agent proposes brand names + domains"). Research only: nothing was registered, bought or signed up for.

**What I looked for:** plain, trustworthy names that work in any state, with no "PLLC" or "NY", not cute and not AI-sounding. Most short one-word .coms are taken (panelready, payerready, credentialingdesk, panelwise, practicepanel and others were registered), so every name below is "[Word] Credentialing". That is also clearer in ads and on a postcard.

**How I checked**

- **.com free:** Verisign RDAP (`rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`). 404 means not registered. All 10 below returned 404 on **Nov 18 2026 at 21:21:47–21:21:48 UTC** (first pass 21:18–21:20 UTC, same result). A name can be taken any minute, so re-check right before buying.
- **Trademark:** USPTO trademark search (tmsearch.uspto.gov), live marks only, classes 35/44 plus coordinated classes, searched on the distinctive word. This is a knockout check, not a clearance opinion.
- **Existing company:** one web search per name for credentialing or medical billing firms.
- **Parked/for sale:** not applicable; none of the 10 .coms is registered.

## Recommended top 3

1. **Fairbank Credentialing** (fairbankcredentialing.com). The cleanest result: nothing on USPTO, no credentialing firm with the name. Reads like a real, settled business, which suits clinicians wary of Headway/Alma-style platforms.
2. **Straightline Credentialing** (straightlinecredentialing.com). The name carries the pitch: flat prices, straight answers. Existing STRAIGHTLINE marks are in unrelated fields.
3. **Clear Panel Credentialing** (clearpanelcredentialing.com). The most self-explanatory for ads and postcards. Check Clearpaths Credentialing first if this is the pick.
