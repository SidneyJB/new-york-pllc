## 5. Comparison with the draft

**Volume (the draft's own concern: demand may cap spend before budget).** The draft estimated roughly 1,500-2,500 in-state commercial searches a month across the five states [E, from S and population share]. Keyword Planner gives, summing the eight core commercial head terms once each (provider credentialing, insurance credentialing, credentialing services, therapist credentialing, medical credentialing services, insurance credentialing services, credentialing company, credentialing services for providers): **about 1,040 a month across the five states** (TX 340, FL 280, NY 230, NJ 100, MD 90). Adding every other on-topic term in the list once raises it to about 1,730, but that total is inflated: about 20 of those rows are the 10 floor and variants overlap. So the observed range is **about 1,000 to 1,700, at or below the low end of the draft's estimate** [E].

**Spend this implies [E, using the draft's assumptions A: 30-50% impression share, about 5% CTR, $25-40 CPC].** 1,000-1,700 searches a month gives roughly 15-85 clicks a month, so about 35-200 clicks over Dec 3 to Feb 14 (73 days). A central case (1,400 searches, 40% share, 5% CTR) is about 28 clicks a month, 67 over the test, about $2,000 at $30. That is **well under $9,000: expect to spend perhaps $1,000-$4,000, not the full budget**, and the 225-360 clicks the draft expected from $9K look out of reach on this vocabulary unless the long tail or a broader keyword set adds a lot. At the draft's 2-5% click-to-paid rate, 35-200 clicks is roughly 1-10 paid clients across all states, which is too few to tell states apart; the channel-level number is the only one the test can read.

**State split.** Draft budgets: TX $2,264 (25%), FL $1,968 (22%), NY $1,700 (19%), MD $1,622 (18%), NJ $1,446 (16%). Share of observed core-term demand [E]: TX 33%, FL 27%, NY 22%, NJ 10%, MD 9%.
