# Credentialing: how much of the market we can realistically take, 2027–2028

> **Correction (Sid, Nov 14 2026): hours per job were overstated.** On Danielle Byrne's case, Sid spent about **30 minutes** of his own time; agents did the rest, at roughly $0 cash. The 20–30 h (first jobs) and 12–18 h (steady state) estimates below assumed a person does the work. They are wrong for how we operate. What changes: capacity is not the binding constraint. ## 1. The answer on one page

**Most likely: credentialing becomes a real but modest line, about 3–4 paid jobs a month across 2027 and about 6 a month in 2028. That is about $55–60K revenue in 2027 and about $110K in 2028, leaving about $20–45K a year in cash after ad spend and a part-time credentialing coordinator's pay (about $40–75K before the coordinator).** It is not the 12–15 jobs a month that the [2028 path-to-$1M plan](../nypllc-2028-path-to-1m-plan.md) §3A needs.

**What drives each case:**
- **Low:** the paid ad test comes back above about $1,500 per client, or under 2 paid from the test. Credentialing then stays a formation add-on fed only by our own customers, at about 1–2 jobs a month. The CAQH pilot's 0 paid from 23 invoices [M] is the base rate for this.
- **Most likely:** our own formation flow gives about 1.5 jobs a month. A one-time campaign to past customers gives about 5. Paid search lands near $800 per client and is held to 2–4 jobs a month by budget and NY search volume. Groups add one or two small accounts a year. Payer closures and our invoice-after-a-written-"open" rule cut the average sale below the $1,670 the model uses.
- **High:** ads come in at $500 or less per client. Headway's $9.99 marketplace fee pushes a visible wave of NY therapists toward their own contracts. Psych NPs, including out-of-state NPs entering NY, become a second engine. 6–10 groups sign up for per-clinician monthly upkeep. This is roughly the bottom of the path plan's lever A, and I would put it at about 1 chance in 6.

**The plain answer to "how much of Medallion's market can we take":** essentially none, because it is not the same market. Medallion sells software plus service per provider to health systems, payers and digital-health employers ([Medallion: $35M Series C, 2022, $350M post-money; $43M in Aug 2025, about $130M raised](https://blog.medallion.co/post/medallion-series-c-35m) [M]; revenue not public [U]). What we can take is a slice of a few hundred NY solo and small-group clinicians a year who pay someone to get in-network. That pool is worth roughly $0.3–1.5M a year in NY behavioral health [E]. Our most likely 2028 is about a tenth of it.

**Where I agree and disagree with the [2028 path plan](../nypllc-2028-path-to-1m-plan.md):**
- **Agree:** its own reality ladder, step 8 ("credentialing ~5 jobs/mo, not 6"; $663K with credentialing). My most likely 2028 (about 6 a month) sits right there.
- **Agree:** the path plan says lead flow, not hours, is the first limit. That is right.
- **Disagree:** lever A (12–15 jobs a month, +$165–210K). In NY behavioral health alone, 150–180 jobs a year would be about 35–40% of the estimated paying pool, against Headway, Grow, Alma, Rula, SimplePractice's free enrollment, national per-payer shops and local consultants. I put it at the high edge (about 1 in 6), not in the path to $1M.
- **Disagree:** the P2-11 model's credentialing line ([P2-11 §1a](p2-11-model-2026-11-11/README.md)) has **no acquisition cost**. Every job beyond our own customers costs ad money; the standalone business exists to win those jobs.
- **Disagree:** the model's $1,670 average job. Danielle's $2,210 is the top of the mix. Closed panels and Foundation-only or Move-my-contracts sales pull the average down.
- **Net effect:** credentialing is worth about **+$45–75K cash in 2028**, not +$165–210K. $1M by 2028 does not run mainly through credentialing. It may run through it in 2029–2030 if groups and recurring revenue work.

**Where we fit:** a NY-specific, done-for-you service for clinicians who just formed or run a PLLC. We are the entity specialist (Type 2 NPI, the PLLC's tax ID, NY Medicaid group enrollment, the PLLC's address), with a trust model (the client only signs; no passwords; everything logged) and a list of buyers who already paid us. **Why a big company's revenue isn't "a pie" for us:**
1. **Different buyer.** Medallion's dollars come from organizations paying per provider per year for software and delegated work. A solo LCSW never enters that market.
2. **The overlap is a threat, not a prize.** Medallion inside SimplePractice offers free enrollment to the same solo therapists we target. The platforms do too (free, in exchange for their contracts and a share of each session).
3. **Venture-funded automation lowers the price of the commodity part** (forms, CAQH, monitoring). What stays paid is the judgment, the follow-up and the entity-specific work, and that's labor-bound. It scales with coordinators, not code.

## 4. Capacity

### 4.1 Hours per job (superseded: see the correction at the top; Sid's own time on the first job was ~30 min)

- **Measured:** 248 credentialing events in the CRM, 9 with minutes (150 minutes total) [M]. Hours per job are not known.
- **What Danielle's case shows [M, from her client file]:** in 9 days the case went through two W-9 reversals, an open question on whether to use her SSN or the PLLC's EIN for Medicaid, a Healthfirst decline (T-8535), Anthem Medicaid and DSNP closed until Apr 1 2027, a Medicaid single-member-group question sent to DOH, two home-address conflicts, a privacy concern about Medicaid publishing her service address, CMS employer approval still pending, and CAQH bulk access still pending. No commercial application has gone in yet. Much of this is first-job learning, but it means **12–14 hours for a full job is not credible for the first 10.**
- **Estimate [E]:** first 10 full jobs 20–30 h each; steady state 12–18 h for setup + 3 commercial payers + Medicaid + Medicare; Foundation 3–4 h; Move my contracts ~5 h; upkeep ~1.5 h/yr.

### 4.2 Payer timelines (do not change with effort or AI)

- NY Insurance Law §4803: a decision within 60 days of a **complete** application [M, per our [panel-status research](../../PLLC-CRM/crm/docs/credentialing/research-panel-status-tracking-2026-11.md)]; typical 60–120 days for commercial [S]; NY Medicaid slower [S]; Medicare 855I usually weeks [S].
- **Panel closures are common for master's-level therapists:** MetroPlus publishes LCSW/LMHC/LMFT closures in all five boroughs [M]; Anthem told us Medicaid/DSNP are closed until Apr 1 2027 [M]; Healthfirst declined our first client [M]; telehealth-only practices are turned away about 5× as often as in-person in one vendor survey [S]. **Psychiatry is rarely on closure lists** [S], and Carelon's settlement with the NY Attorney General (AOD 26-007, effective Nov 10 2026) requires it to recruit NY psychiatrists, psych NPs and therapists [M].
- **What this does to cash:** start to first payer decision is about 3–5 months [E], so testimonials and referrals start around mid-2027. The new rule (invoice an insurer only after a written "open" from that payer) is honest and protects against refunds, but it delays revenue and lowers the average ticket. I built that into the $1,400–1,450 average.

### 4.3 What the two 2027 hires change

| | Before hires | Ops coordinator (~10–15 h/wk) | Credentialing coordinator (~20 h/wk, part-time) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who sells | Sid | Sid gets 10–15 h/wk back for sales and group calls | Sid |
| Who delivers | Sid, up to ~5–7 jobs/mo (path plan) | Same | ~5–6 full jobs/mo at 15 h each, plus follow-ups and upkeep |
| Cash cost | $0 | ~$27K/yr (path plan; charged to formation) | ~$34K/yr (20 h × $30 × 52 + ~8% payroll tax) [A] |
| Break-even | — | — | ~2–3 non-ad jobs/mo (~$1,320 each) or ~5–6 ad-sourced jobs/mo (~$520 each) of contribution [E] |

**My read:** the binding limit through 2027 is demand and Sid's selling time, not delivery hours. Hire the credentialing coordinator **part-time when the trigger hits** (about 5 jobs a month for two months, as the path plan says), not on a date. The ops coordinator matters more for credentialing than it looks, because Sid's freed hours are what sells groups.

### 4.4 AI and automation: what is realistic

- **It helps with:** intake extraction from documents, CAQH bulk upload files, prefilled NPPES/ETIN/855I worksheets, status reminders, drafting payer emails, and the panel-status log. Expected effect: **20–35% fewer hours on paperwork** [A].
- **It doesn't help with:** payer review clocks, phone holds with provider relations, notary visits, client signatures and attestations, closed panels, and judgment calls (which tax ID, which address). Those are most of the elapsed time and much of the labor [E].
- **It doesn't make us different:** Medallion, Assured and Verifiable all market AI agents and are funded to build them [M]. AI raises our margin; it doesn't win clients.

## 6. Risks and the case against the plan

### 6.1 The strongest case against (argued as hard as I can)

1. **The base rate is bad.** Our only earlier credentialing product sold 0 from 23+ invoices [M]. The new offer has one sale, from someone who asked for help after trying DIY. One client is an anecdote, not a conversion rate.
2. **The best-funded players are giving away the commodity part to the same buyers:** Headway, Grow and Rula for free on platform contracts [S]; SimplePractice with Medallion for its subscribers [M]. Our price has to be justified by entity-specific work and our own contracts, not by "we do the forms."
3. **Panels are closed exactly where our volume is** (master's-level therapists, NYC and Long Island, telehealth). Our first client already has 2 of 6 payers declined or closed [M]. Honest invoicing after a written "open" means fewer billable payers per client.
4. **Our ads have never been tested for this.** The standalone site's whole case rests on ad cost per client, which is unmeasured. At $1,500 per client, ad-sourced solo jobs lose money once labor is paid.
5. **The work is labor-bound and slow to prove.** Results arrive 3–5 months after the sale; the first job is running well over the 12-hour estimate; and the coordinator's pay is a fixed cost before volume is proven.
6. **The Headway effect may be small.** The fee applies only to marketplace-sourced clients [S], Headway paused the contract rollout [S], and therapists who leave give up Headway's referral flow and possibly its rates [A]. The exodus may be a news story, not a market.
7. **NY's pool is small.** About 450 buyers a year [E]. Above about 6 jobs a month we need share no new NY entrant should expect, or new niches and states where we have no edge.

### 6.2 Why I still land at "most likely ~6 jobs a month in 2028," not lower

- The demand we have is real and unprompted: Danielle, Gabriella, Lauren Hanna, Dunia Gibson, the psych NP group questions, and about 20% of checkouts ticking the box [M]. The offer now sells the outcome (in-network), which fixes the pilot's main mistake.
- Our formation list is a free, renewing channel the national shops don't have: ~157 behavioral-health PLLCs a year [M], each reached at the moment of need.
- Psych NPs and groups don't face the platform and closure problems that master's-level solos do.
- Taking about 10% of a NY pool in year two, through an owned funnel plus modest ads, is ambitious but ordinary for a specialist [A].

### 6.3 Failure modes to watch

- Hours per job stay above 20 after the first 10 jobs → raise prices, narrow scope, or decline Medicaid-group cases.
- Refunds or credits for closed panels go above 15% of revenue → separate the network check (a small fee, credited toward the package).
- A data incident (SSNs, DOBs) → stop selling until fixed; get E&O and cyber cover before the coordinator starts [A].
- Sid's formation work slips (close-outs, mail station) as credentialing grows → the ops hire comes first.

### 6.4 Leading indicators, monthly

| Indicator | Healthy | Alarm |

### 6.5 Kill and scale criteria

- **Mar 31 2027 (the path plan's gate):** **Scale** (hire the part-time coordinator, raise ad budget toward $3–4K/mo) if paid jobs average ≥ 4/mo over Jan–Mar, median hours ≤ 18, and at least 3 payer effective dates are in hand. **Keep as an add-on, with no standalone ad spend beyond the test**, if it is 2–4/mo. **Shrink to formation customers only** if under 2/mo.
- **Ad test (6–8 weeks, $2–3K):** continue only if ad cost per paid client is ≤ $1,000 with at least 3 paid. Stop paid ads if it is above $1,500 or under 2 paid.
- **Sep 30 2027:** if no group account has signed, drop the group thesis for 2028 planning and plan around about 4 solo jobs a month.
