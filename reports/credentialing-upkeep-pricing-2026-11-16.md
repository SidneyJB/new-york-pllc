A solo client who later adds clinicians moves to the retainer at the third clinician.

## What upkeep includes

Per clinician, for the insurers we enrolled or linked for them (and any they already hold under the PLLC):

1. **CAQH re-attestation every 120 days.** We update the profile and replace expiring documents; the clinician clicks attest (CAQH reserves that step for the provider).
2. **Revalidations:** Medicaid (every 5 years in NY, eMedNY) and Medicare (855 revalidation when CMS posts the due date).
3. **Address, roster and directory updates:** practice address or phone changes across NPPES (due within 30 days of a change), CAQH and each insurer; adding or removing a location; a yearly check that each insurer's directory lists the clinician correctly.
4. **Recredentialing** with each contracted insurer when it comes due (usually every 3 years), included at no extra charge.
5. Reminders before license, malpractice and (prescribers) DEA expire, with the updated document filed everywhere it is needed.

Not included: new insurers (priced per insurer), closed-panel appeals (add-on, price not yet set), billing or claims work, and new clinicians (group retainer or a new package).

## $249/yr vs $69/$119

- **$249/yr (about $21/month)** is below every published solo maintenance price found. The only cheaper figure is PPS's $30–60, and that is a custom group quote. At $21/month the line can't carry recurring revenue (the upside-paths report counted ~$8K/yr at 30–35 subscribers).
- **$69 for therapists** sits under Advance A Practice's $80 Essentials while including recredentialing, which they charge $200 per plan for. It is still a clear step down from the $99–129 group rate, so a solo clinician who grows into a group sees the per-clinician price rise only modestly.
- **$119 for prescribers** reflects more enrollments to keep current (Medicare, Medicaid, 4 commercial insurers in the $2,995 package, plus DEA). The nearest like-for-like figure, Advance A Practice Complete for 6 insurers, is $145/month; RCM Matter is $199.
- **Our cash cost is about $0** per client plus card fees. Labor isn't counted; Sid's review time runs about 30 minutes per job, and upkeep work is mostly agent-prepared. The price is set by the market and by attach rate, not by cost.
- **Risk:** at 3x the old price, attach may fall below the 35% target. Watch the first 10 package sales; if fewer than 3 add upkeep, test $49 for therapists before cutting anything else.
