# 02 · Professional-entity formation rules in 20 states other than NY

Research date: Nov 18 2026. Brief: [README.md](README.md). This file covers brief sections 5 (what it legally takes), 6 (the work itself) and 7 (recurrence). Sections 3, 4, 8 and 9 belong to another agent.

Tags: `[M]` = I opened the primary source (statute, agency page or agency form) · `[S]` = secondary source or search snippet · `[U]` = looked for it but could not find or open it. `[CALC]` = arithmetic on source figures.

**How this was gathered, and its limits.** The session's shared web-search budget ran out after 3 searches, so most pages were reached by typing known agency or statute URLs directly. Many state sites refused the fetch, so their figures are `[U]`:

- 403 or Cloudflare blocks: CA leginfo, FL Sunbiz, IL SOS, GA SOS, TN SOS, OH SOS, MA BoRM, Justia.
- Certificate errors: ILGA, CT General Assembly.
- JavaScript-only or Incapsula pages: TX statutes, NV SOS.
- 404s: CO SOS, CT SOTS, MD SDAT and WA SOS fee pages, at the URLs tried.

Section numbers listed in the "not verified" lists are leads to check. They are **not** verified facts.

## State notes

### New Jersey
- **Fees** [M] ([Registry fee schedule](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml), "Last Updated: Wednesday, 07/01/26"):
  - Domestic LLC certificate of formation $100. Domestic for-profit corporation $100.
  - Annual report $75 for LLCs and corporations.
  - Expedite, over the counter only: per filing $15 (corp) or $25 (LLC); same-day fax filing $50 (LLC); 2-hour $500; 1-hour $1,000.
- **Online filing** [M] ([Getting registered](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/gettingregistered.shtml)): the certificate of formation and NJ-REG can be filed online. The page says "The fee is $100 for all for-profit entities." [Business formation portal](https://www.njportal.com/DOR/BusinessFormation/) lists "LLC, PA, DP".
- **Not opened:** the professional corporation statute (N.J.S.A. 14A:17), the RULLCA professional-services provisions (N.J.S.A. 42:2C), the Board of Medical Examiners entity rules, and the court rules for law-firm entities. All [U].

### Connecticut

### Pennsylvania
- **Fees** [M] ([PA DOS Fees and Payments](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/programs/business/fees-and-payments), no page date, ©2026):
  - Domestic LLC certificate of organization $125. Domestic business corporation articles $125.
  - Annual report (for-profit) $7.
  - "Annual Registration (restricted professional companies only)": "at least $500". The page does not say whether this is per member.
  - Expedite: same-day (received by 10 a.m.) $100; 3-hour (by 2 p.m.) $300; 1-hour (by 4 p.m.) $1,000. Expedited requests are not accepted by mail.
- **Not opened** [U]: the professional corporation and restricted professional company chapters of 15 Pa.C.S. The URL tried returned "Error VS-8". Also not opened: the corporate advertising (publication) requirement, said to be 15 Pa.C.S. §1307.

### Massachusetts
- **M.G.L. c.156A §3** [M] ([link](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter156A/Section3)): a PC may be organized for one profession, or for two or more professions unless the licensing laws or boards expressly prohibit it.
- **c.156A §5** [M] ([link](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter156A/Section5)): a PC renders services "only through its officers, employees and agents who are duly licensed."
- Neither section mentions a board certificate.
- **Not opened** [U]: the c.156C professional-LLC rule (§2 has no professional-services definition [M]). The SOC fee page returned no amounts. The Board of Registration in Medicine entity-registration page returned 403.

### Florida
- **Fla. Stat. ch. 621 (2026)** [M] ([link](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0621/0621.html)):
  - PCs are formed under ch. 607 (§621.05). PLLCs are formed under ch. 605 (§621.051).
  - Owners must be licensed for "the same specific professional services". PCs and PLLCs may also be owners (§621.09). Transfers are limited to eligible owners (§621.11).
  - Names: a PC uses "chartered", "P.A." or "PA"; a PLLC formed on or after 1/1/2014 uses "PLLC" (§621.12).
  - The chapter text contains no licensing-board approval step.
- **Fees** [M]:
  - [§605.0213](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0605/Sections/0605.0213.html): LLC articles $100, RA designation $25, annual report $50.
  - [§607.0122](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0607/Sections/0607.0122.html): corp articles $35, RA designation $35, annual report $61.25, supplemental corporate fee $88.75.
  - Corp annual report total: $61.25 + $88.75 = $150.00 [CALC].
- **Not opened** [U]: whether a supplemental fee also applies to the LLC annual report. Sunbiz fee pages returned 403.

### Texas

- **Not opened** [U]: BOC chapters 301–304 (the statute site renders by script only), Texas Medical Board entity pages (404), and the SOS expedite fee.

### California
- **SOS** [M]:
  - [Corporations, domestic](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/forms/corporations-california-domestic): Articles of Incorporation, Professional (ARTS-PC), $100. ### Illinois
- **IDFPR PLLC guide** [M] ([PDF](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/idfpr/forms/online/-248-limited-liability-company-user-guide.pdf)):
  - The applicant uploads the "Articles of Organization for PLLC as filed with the Illinois Secretary of State". So the IDFPR registration comes **after** the SOS filing.
  - "The registration fee is $50.00." Registrations "expire January 1 on a three‐year renewal cycle".
  - The application is filed online.
  - All members and managers must be licensed for the same profession for: dentistry, medicine, social work, clinical professional counseling or professional counseling, veterinary, clinical psychology, marriage and family therapy, and sex-offender evaluation.
  - One application per business location.
  - Law firms apply to the Illinois Supreme Court Clerk instead. Accounting and design firms file their own IDFPR firm applications after the SOS filing.
- **IDFPR pages** [M] ([medical corporation](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/profs/medicalcorp.html), [professional service corporation](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/profs/pfsrvcp.html)): both registrations exist and are applied for online. Fees are not shown.
- **Not opened** [U]: SOS LLC and corporation fees (403) and the ILGA statute text (certificate error).

### Michigan
- **MCL 450.4201** [M] ([link](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-4201)): an LLC "formed to provide services in a learned profession" "shall comply with article 9."
- **MCL 450.4904** [M] ([link](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-4904)):
  - For Public Health Code professions, all members and managers must be licensed for "the same professional service".
  - Chiropractors, physicians, osteopaths and podiatrists may combine. PAs may join them; a PA-only company is barred.
  - Architecture, engineering and surveying: a PLLC needs all members licensed in Michigan; an ordinary LLC needs two-thirds.
  - Public accounting: more than 50% of equity must be held by authorized persons.
  - The section names no board approval step.
- **MCL 450.5101** [M] ([link](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-5101)): LLC articles $50. Annual statement $25 through Sep 30 2027, then $15. An extra $50 applies to documents filed by fax or electronic transmission.

### Ohio
- **R.C. ch. 1785** [M] ([link](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/chapter-1785)):
  - Professional associations are available to listed professions, including medicine, dentistry, nursing, psychology, counseling, social work, MFT, law, accounting, architecture and engineering.
  - Shareholders must be licensed for the same service, with listed combined-practice exceptions.
  - The chapter has no board certificate step.
  - §1785.09: the chapter does not bar professional services through an LLC under ch. 1705 or 1706.
- **R.C. 1785.06** [M] ([link](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1785.06)): a biennial statement listing shareholders is due within 30 days after June 30 of each even-numbered year. If it is not filed, the articles are cancelled after notice.
- **R.C. 111.16** [M] ([link](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-111.16)):
  - Articles of incorporation: minimum $99 (tiered by shares; maximum $100,000).
  - LLC articles: $99.
  - Biennial statement: $25 (division (I)(2)).
- **Not opened** [U]: expedite fees (SOS page returned 403).

### North Carolina
- **G.S. 55B-4** [M] ([link](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_55B/GS_55B-4.html)):
  - At least one incorporator, director and officer must be a licensee. All shares must be held by licensees (except as §55B-6 allows).
  - The articles must include a certification from the licensing board that share ownership complies.
- **G.S. 57D-2-02** [M] ([link](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_57D/GS_57D-2-02.html)): a PLLC is subject to ch. 55B. The name must include "PLLC" or "Professional".
- **NC Medical Board PLLC page** [M] ([link](https://www.ncmedboard.org/licensing-registration/licensing/corporations/professional-limited-liability-companies)):
  - Steps: online application with the $50 fee; then the notarized Certificate of Organizing Members (PLLC-01); then the Board emails sealed Articles and certificate (PLLC-02); then the SOS filing, by mail or SOS online upload.
  - The SOS filing "must be completed within four months of the NCMB Certificate issued date."
  - Physician + PA and PA-only PLLCs are allowed. The page does not mention NPs.
  - No processing time is published.
- **NCMB fee schedule** [M] ([PDF](https://www.ncmedboard.org/images/uploads/other_pdfs/NCMB_Fee_Schedule_08212026.pdf), "last updated 08.21.2026"): PC, PLLC and foreign PC/PLLC each pay $50 application, $25 annual renewal and $10 late fee.
- **SOS fees** [M] ([link](https://www.sosnc.gov/fees/by_title/_Business_Registration)):
  - Articles: $125 (corporation or LLC).
  - Annual report: corporation $25; LLC $200.
  - Expedite: 24-hour $100; same-day $200 (received by noon).
- **Not opened** [U]: entity steps at other NC boards (Psychology, Social Work, LCMHC, Nursing, Dental).

### Georgia

### Virginia
- **Va. Code §13.1-1101.1** [M] ([link](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter13/section13.1-1101.1/)): professional services may be rendered by an LLC organized under Chapter 12, i.e. an ordinary LLC, "Unless otherwise prohibited by law or regulation".
- **§13.1-1102** [M] ([link](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter13/section13.1-1102/)):
  - The covered professions include practitioners of the healing arts, APRNs, behavioral science professions, dentists, attorneys, CPAs and design professions.
  - Healing-arts and behavioral-science PLLCs: all members must be licensed.
  - Design professions: two-thirds of interests must be held by Virginia licensees.
- **§13.1-1113** [M] ([link](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter13/section13.1-1113/)): a law PLLC "shall first obtain and maintain a registration certificate" under Title 54.1 ch. 39 before practicing.
- **§13.1-549** [M] ([link](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter7/section13.1-549/)): design-profession corporations need a certificate of authority from the APELSCIDLA Board.
- **Fees** [M]: LLC articles $100 ([§13.1-1005](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter12/section13.1-1005/)). Annual registration fee $50 ([§13.1-1062](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter12/section13.1-1062/)).
- **Not opened** [U]: SCC fee page (404) and VSB registration fee.

### Maryland
- **Corps. & Ass'ns §5-102** [M] ([link](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gca&section=5-102&enactments=false)):
  - A PC exists "solely for the purpose of rendering professional services within a single profession" (multi-profession allowed for related services).
  - A business eligible to be a PC "may not organize under any other corporate form permitted by this article". Architects, engineers, real estate licensees and veterinarians are exempt from that restriction.
- **§4A-201** [M]: an LLC may be formed for "any lawful business" except insurance. The section does not address professional services.
- **Not opened** [U]: SDAT fees (404).

### Washington
- **RCW 25.15.046** [M] ([link](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=25.15.046)):
  - Persons licensed to render the same professional services may form a PLLC, which is subject to RCW 18.100.
  - If members must be licensed and the PLLC lacks professional liability insurance or financial responsibility of at least $1M (or the insurance commissioner's figure), members may be personally liable.
  - No licensing-authority certificate is required by the text.
- **RCW 18.100** [M] ([link](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=18.100&full=true)):
  - §.050: licensed individuals are the incorporators. §.050(5) lets listed health professions combine in one corporation.
  - §.090: stock only to licensed individuals (or qualified trusts).
  - §.120: a shareholder list is filed with the first annual report and later filings.
- **Not opened** [U]: SOS fees (404).

### Arizona
- **A.R.S. 29-3201(G)** [M] ([link](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/29/03201.htm)): within 60 days after the articles are filed, either a notice is published three consecutive times in a newspaper in the statutory agent's county, or the commission enters the filing in its database (only if the agent's county has more than 800,000 people).
- **A.R.S. 10-2220** [M] ([link](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/10/02220.htm)): PC voting shares go to licensed persons and listed entities. Others may hold up to 49% in aggregate "unless the licensing authority sets a different percentage."
- **Not opened** [U]: ACC fees (404) and the professional-LLC provisions.

### Minnesota
- **Minn. Stat. ch. 319B** [M] ([link](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/319B/full)):
  - Corporations, LLCs and LLPs may elect into the Act in their organizational document (§319B.03 subd. 2).
  - Ownership is limited to licensed professionals and listed entities (§319B.07).
  - §319B.40: medicine, PA, nursing, psychology, social work, MFT, dentistry, pharmacy, podiatry, etc. may combine.

### Nevada
- **NRS 89.040(1)(c)** [M] ([link](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-089.html)): the articles must include a certificate from the regulating board showing that each natural-person owner or manager is licensed.
- **NRS 89.050**: one specific type of professional service, with listed combinations.
- **NRS 89.070**: interests go only to natural persons licensed for the same service.
- **NRS 89.250**: professional associations file an initial and annual list, $150.

  - NRS 86.561: LLC articles $75.

## Non-attorney preparation of these filings (UPL / document-preparer rules)

- **Nevada** [M] (NRS ch. 240A, [link](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-240A.html)):
  - A "document preparation service" is a person who, for compensation and at a client's direction, prepares documents or submits them to an agency in a "legal matter" (§240A.030, .040).
  - "Legal matter" includes any filing that affects a person's legal rights or obligations.
  - The chapter does not name business-entity filings.
  - Registration: $100 application, $50 annual renewal, $25,000 bond (higher tiers for entities with multiple registrants) (§§240A.100, .110, .120–.126).
- **All other 19 states** [U]: no UPL opinion or document-preparer rule on business-entity filings was found or opened.
  - Not opened: CA Bus. & Prof. Code §6400 (Legal Document Assistant), AZ ACJA §7-208, FL Bar Rule 10-2.1, and the TX UPL Committee materials.
