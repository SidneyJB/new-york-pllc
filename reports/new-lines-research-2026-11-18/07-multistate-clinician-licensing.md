## Cross-cutting volume data (applies to lines a–d)

### Physicians (FSMB census, IMLC)
- 2024 FSMB census: **1,082,187** actively licensed physicians holding **1,671,477** licenses. Shares: **76%** hold one license, **16%** two, **9% three or more** (census gives percentages, not counts). `[M]` https://www.jmronline.org/content/111/2/7 (J Med Regulation 111(2), census year 2024, published 2025)
  - 9% × 1,082,187 ≈ 97,400 physicians with 3+ licenses `[CALC]` (percentage is rounded in source, so the count is approximate).
- New licenses issued by state medical boards in 2024: **≈146,000** (record). Of these, 109,498 to US grads, 12,758 to US-citizen IMGs, 23,597 to non-US IMGs; **32,989** were first-time full unrestricted licenses. `[M]` same source.
  - 146,000 − 32,989 ≈ 113,000 licenses went to physicians who already held a license somewhere `[CALC]` (the census labels the 32,989 as "first-time"; it does not label the remainder "out-of-state", so treat the subtraction as a proxy only).
- IMLC: licenses issued via IMLC 1 Apr 2024–31 Mar 2025 = **20,804** from **8,400+** applications (as reported in the census article). `[M]` jmronline as above.
- IMLCC Year-8 data study (released Jul 3 2025, completed applications 1 Apr 2024–31 Mar 2025): **8,449** completed applications; average **4.0** licenses per applicant; **38%** obtained 3+ licenses, **14%** 7+; 7% found ineligible; average **37 days** application → Letter of Qualification (52% within 30 days); average **20 days** LOQ → licenses issued. Cumulative since Apr 2017: 42,000+ physicians, 95,000+ applications, 150,000+ licenses, 41 states + DC + Guam. States with most LOQ applications: TX, FL, OH, NJ, IL. States issuing most IMLC licenses: TX, WI, AL, IL, AZ. `[M]` https://imlcc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/IMLCC__Year8_DataStudy-7-3-2025-FINAL.pdf
- IMLCC snapshot (Apr 16 2025): 43 member states, 56 boards, 90,000+ LOQs, 140,000+ licenses; "30% of all new licenses are issued through the IMLCC process in member-states"; "Coming soon – Enhanced physician portal & **delegate access** … Delegates can manage the application process, renewals". `[M]` https://imlcc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FSMB-Handouts-Snapshot-Stats-April-2025.pdf
- IMLCC FY2025 annual report (Jul 2024–Jun 2025) headline "170,000+ licenses / 90,000+ LOQs / 42 states" — `[S]` (search snippet of https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2026/mandated/260218.pdf; PDF text not extractable).

## Line (a) — Done-for-you additional-state license applications (incl. compacts, telehealth registrations)

### 1. What it is and who buys it
- Each state requires its own license (or a compact privilege / telehealth registration) to treat a patient located in that state. Examples: FL s. 456.47(4) lets non-FL-licensed professionals provide telehealth to FL patients only if registered; registrant "may not open an office in this state and may not provide in-person health care services" (s. 456.47(4)(f)). `[M]` https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/456.47
- Buyers named by providers: physicians (locum, telehealth), NPs/PAs, RNs, behavioral-health therapists (LCSW, LPC, LMFT, LMHC, psychologists) `[M]` DirectShifts page below.

### 5. What it legally takes to offer it
- **No state licensing of "license expediters" for health-professional licensing was found** `[U]` (searched; no statute located).
- Third-party preparation is anticipated by some boards, with applicant attestation required:
  - Texas Medical Board "Third Party Completion Affidavit" (rev. 07/2022): applicant states "I did not complete the application form myself; it was completed for me by a third party," declares the original attestation invalid and re-affirms under penalty of perjury. `[S]` (search summary of tmb.state.tx.us/dl/… forms). TMB "Authorization and Release" form lets the applicant name a "Third-Party Staff" member to discuss the application `[M]` (field "Name of Third-Party Staff" seen in TMB form PDF, tmb.texas.gov/node/3314).
  - Florida (Board of Clinical Social Work/MFT/MHC endorsement application, 2020 PDF): "I, the undersigned, state that I am the person identified in this application… providing false information may result in disciplinary action… or criminal penalties pursuant to s. 456.067"; "You may print this application and sign it or sign digitally"; HIV/AIDS affidavit must be **notarized** if course not yet done. `[M]` https://floridasmentalhealthprofessions.gov/applications/app-licensure-by-endorsement-current.pdf . FL MQA lets third parties pay applicant fees via an "Other Payer Code" (Apr 28 2025 notice) `[S]`.
  - California BBS LCSW Path A (rev. 06/2026): "Sign the application in ink (wet signature) or electronically… via an electronic signature platform such as Adobe Sign or DocuSign"; "Knowingly making a false statement… may be grounds for denial". `[M]` https://bbs.ca.gov/pdf/forms/lcs/lcsapp_oos.pdf
  - California BRN: advanced-practice verification forms "will not be accepted from applicants" — must come from school/certifier `[M]` https://www.rn.ca.gov/applicants/ad-pract.shtml
  - IMLC: delegate access to the physician portal announced as "coming soon" (Apr 2025) `[M]`; current status `[U]`.
- Data-security duties when holding SSNs etc.:
  - NY GBL § 899-bb(2)(a): any person or business that owns or licenses computerized data including private information of a NY resident must "develop, implement and maintain reasonable safeguards". `[M]` https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/899-BB (SSN inclusion is in § 899-aa definition, not opened `[U]`).
  - FL § 501.171, TX Bus. & Com. Code § 521.052, CA Civ. Code § 1798.81.5 (comparable duties): `[U]` not opened.
  - NPDB: a self-query can only be submitted by the practitioner on themself — "Self-Queries submitted on anyone other than yourself will be rejected." `[M]` https://www.npdb.hrsa.gov/faqs/sq30.jsp . NPDB confidentiality rules for redisclosure by a vendor holding a practitioner's self-query: `[U]`.
  - HIPAA applicability to a licensing vendor: `[U]` not researched.
