## (a) NYS Liquor Authority (SLA)

### 1. What it is / who buys
- Retail on-premises, off-premises, manufacturing and wholesale licenses; temporary permits (operating permit while an application is pending); alterations ("change" applications for ownership, layout, method of operation); special-event permits. [M] https://sla.ny.gov/get-license (4 license groups; temp permits "for on-premises licenses and grocery stores while a license application is pending")
- Requirement: no alcohol sales without the license/original certificate. [M] https://sla.ny.gov/what-you-need-know-become-licensed-retailer ("Do not serve or sell alcohol until you receive the original certificate").

### 5. What it takes to offer it
- SLA applicant may use counsel: 9 NYCRR 52.3 "The applicant for a license may be represented by counsel," the attorney "shall be a member of the bar of the State of New York in good standing" (Part 52, hearing after disapproval of application). [M via Cornell page fetched] https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/9-NYCRR-52.3 ; 52.2 (applicant appears in person at hearing) seen only in snippet [S].
- Paid third parties: SLA on-premises application instructions (rev 07/30/25): "Section 166 of the Executive Law requires a regulatory agency to maintain for public inspection a record of who appears before it for a fee as a third party (i.e. an attorney, an agent, a lobbyist or representative) on behalf of a person ... subject to the regulatory jurisdiction of the agency." "This form [Notice of Appearance] must be completed and submitted by all represented applicants." "If you were assisted in the preparation and/or filing of the application, you must have the person you paid complete and submit the Notice of Appearance with the application." [M] https://sla.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/01/on-premises-instructions-nybe_12192025.pdf
- Executive Law s166 (record of appearances; paraphrase, text exceeds quote limit): every listed regulatory agency (includes State Liquor Authority, Dept of Agriculture and Markets, Dept of Education, Dept of Health, Dept of State) keeps a public record of attorneys/agents/representatives appearing for a fee; filed quarterly (15th day after quarter-end) with the Temporary State Commission on Lobbying; Division of Corporations and State Records excluded; no penalty stated in section. [M] https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/EXC/166
- Instructions line 2: applicant lists "the attorney, representative or contact person for the completion, filing and handling" of the application; notices of incomplete applications go to applicant and attorney/representative. [M] same PDF.

### 6. The work
- Online application via New York Business Express (print copy to submit); municipal 30-day notice (on-premises; Community Board in NYC, clerk elsewhere); two weekly newspaper notices; posting within 10 days of filing; fingerprinting (electronic); deficiency letter response 10 business days; Full Board or Licensing Board determination; original license mailed/picked up at SLA offices. 200-ft and 500-ft laws; 500-foot hearing; disapproval hearing or reconsideration. [M] https://sla.ny.gov/what-you-need-know-become-licensed-retailer
- Processing: "Most license applications take about 22 to 26 weeks"; temporary permits ~30 days, valid 180 days (renewable 30 more days). [M] https://sla.ny.gov/get-license and the retailer page.
- Fees: SLA Schedule of Retail License Fees (rev 6/12/26) [M] https://sla.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/06/retail-fee-chart_6122026.pdf . Examples: On-Premises Liquor (OP 252) 2-yr: $4,352 (NY, Kings, Bronx, Queens) / $3,072 (Richmond; Buffalo, Rochester, Syracuse, Yonkers) / $2,432 (Albany, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, Niagara Falls, Schenectady, White Plains, Utica) / $1,792 elsewhere, + $200 non-refundable filing fee. Liquor Store (L 222) 3-yr: $4,098 / $2,562 / $1,536 / $1,536 + $200. Restaurant Wine (RW 341) 2-yr $960/$960/$480/$480 + $100. Eating Place Beer 3-yr $960/$960/$480/$480 + $100. Wine Store 3-yr $1,920/$960/$435/$435 + $100. Catering (CT 246) $2,902/$2,048/$1,622/$1,194 + $200. Each additional bar: same fee + $20 filing. Seasonal and additional-bar tables exist in the chart.

### 7. Recurrence
- License terms on the chart: 1, 2 or 3 years by class (OP 2-yr; liquor store, beer 3-yr). 23,805 renewal applications in 2024; $40.66M renewal fees. [M]
