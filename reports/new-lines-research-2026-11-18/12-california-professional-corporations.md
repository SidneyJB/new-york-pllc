## 1. Law

### 1.1 Who must use a professional corporation (not an LLC)

**Corp. Code §17701.04** `[S-mirror]` ([mirror](https://california.public.law/codes/corporations_code_section_17701.04))
- (b): an LLC may render licensed services only "if the applicable provisions of the Business and Professions Code, the Chiropractic Act, the Osteopathic Act, or the Yacht and Ship Brokers Act authorize a limited liability company … to hold that license, certificate, or registration."
- (e): "Nothing in this title shall be construed to permit a domestic or foreign limited liability company to render professional services, as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 13401 and in Section 13401.3, in this state."

**Corp. Code §13401(a)** `[S-mirror]`: "'Professional services' means any type of professional services that may be lawfully rendered only pursuant to a license, certification, or registration authorized by the Business and Professions Code, the Chiropractic Act, or the Osteopathic Act."

**§13401(b)** `[S-mirror]`
- A professional corporation (PC) renders services "in a single profession, except as otherwise authorized in Section 13401.5, pursuant to a certificate of registration issued by the governmental agency regulating the profession."
- **Exempt from the certificate of registration.** A PC whose services are rendered by licensees of these boards "shall not be required to obtain a certificate of registration":
  - Medical Board (and its examining committees)
  - Podiatric Medical Board
  - Osteopathic Medical Board
  - Dental Board
  - Dental Hygiene Board
  - Board of Pharmacy
  - Veterinary Medical Board
  - California Architects Board
  - Court Reporters Board
  - Board of Behavioral Sciences (BBS)
  - Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology and Hearing Aid Dispensers Board
  - Board of Registered Nursing (BRN)
  - State Board of Optometry
  - Board of Occupational Therapy
- **Not on the exempt list**, among the boards in scope: State Bar, Board of Accountancy (CBA), Board of Chiropractic Examiners, Board of Psychology, Physical Therapy Board.

**§13404** `[S-mirror]`
- The articles "shall contain a specific statement that the corporation is a professional corporation within the meaning of this part."
- Except as provided in §13401(b), "no professional corporation shall render professional services in this state without a currently effective certificate of registration issued by the governmental agency regulating the profession."

**Other Moscone-Knox sections** `[S-mirror]`
- **§13403:** a one-shareholder PC needs one director, who is president and treasurer. "The other officers of the corporation in such situation need not be licensed persons."
- **§13405:** services are rendered only through licensed employees.
- **§13406(a):** shares may be issued only to licensed persons. Shares issued otherwise are void. Voting trusts and proxies to non-shareholders are void.
- **§13407:**
  - Shares may be transferred only to licensed persons, shareholders or a PC.
  - A disqualified shareholder's shares must be transferred within 90 days; a deceased shareholder's within 6 months.
  - If not, the certificate of registration "may be suspended or revoked."

**Engineers (B&P §6738)** `[S-mirror]`: civil, electrical and mechanical engineers may practice as a "sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability partnership, firm, or corporation". The condition is that a California-licensed engineer is "an owner, partner, or officer in charge of the engineering practice." A non-licensee may be a partner or officer, but not sole owner. No PC is required by this section.

### 1.3 Name rules by profession `[S-mirror]` unless noted

- **General (Corp. Code §13409):** a PC "may adopt any name permitted by a law expressly applicable to the profession … or by a rule or regulation of the governmental agency regulating that profession." The SOS may require proof by affidavit.
- **Medical:**
  - B&P §2285: using "any name other than his or her own … as the name of a professional corporation" without a Medical Board fictitious-name permit "constitutes unprofessional conduct".
  - §2415(b)(3): the name must not be "deceptive, misleading, or confusing".
  - The MBC page cites CCR 1350.3 for similarity to names already issued `[M]`.
- **Osteopathic** (OMBC FAQ quoting 16 CCR §1676) `[M]`:
  - The name must contain "medical group," "medical clinic," "medical corporation," "medical associates," "medical center," or "medical office."
  - "If you are using your name for a corporation, you must have a Fictitious Name Permit … Adding the corporate designation is what triggers the requirement."
- **Nursing (§2778):** the name must contain "nursing" or "registered nursing" plus a corporate designation.
- **Psychological (§2998):** the name must contain one of the words in "subdivision (c) of Section 2902" plus a corporate designation. The current §2902(c) on the mirror defines "Board", and the word list is in §2902(d). This is a cross-reference mismatch in the text as read.
- **LMFT (§4987.7):** one of "marriage," "family," or "child" together with one of "counseling," "counselor," "therapy," or "therapist". The patient must be told before treatment that the business is an MFT corporation.
- **LCSW (§4998.2):** the name must contain "licensed clinical social worker" plus a corporate designation, with the same patient notice.
- **LPCC (§4999.125):** "licensed professional clinical counselor" or "professional clinical counselor" plus a corporate designation, with the same patient notice.
- **Dental:**
  - §1804: "dental corporation" or corporate-existence wording.
  - §1701.5(b)(4): a fictitious name must contain "dental group," "dental practice," "dental office," or "dental corporation."
- **Physical therapy (§2693):** "physical therapy" or "physical therapist" plus a corporate designation.
- **Optometric (§3163):** limited to the name or last name of present, prospective or former shareholders, plus "optometric corporation" or a corporate designation, except under a §3078 permit.
- **Architectural (§5610.3):** names of shareholders or predecessors, plus "architectural corporation" or "architect(s)" with a corporate designation.
- **Law (§6171(c)–(d)):** the name must comply with State Bar rules. A fictitious name permit is required "when required by the rules and regulations". The State Bar page applies Rule of Professional Conduct 7.1(a) and Rule 3.154(B) `[M]`.

## 3. Secretary of State

**Articles (ARTS-PC)** `[M]` ([SOS corporations forms](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/forms/corporations-california-domestic))
- Fee $100.
- Filed online through bizfile Online ("5 days or less") or by PDF ("10+ days").
- Name reservation: $10.

**Expedite** `[M]` ([Service Options](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/service-options))
- 24-hour (Class C): $350, online or drop-off.
- Same-day (Class B): $750, online or drop-off. The document must be received by 9:30 a.m.
- 4-hour (Class A): $500, drop-off in Sacramento only, and the document "must be precleared and approved".
- 24-hour preclearance: $500, drop-off only.
- Drop-off special handling: $15 per filing request.

**Current processing** `[M]` ([Processing Times](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/processing-times), last updated Nov 18 2026). The SOS shows the date of the requests it is now working on:

Lag on corporate formations as of Nov 18: online 8 days, counter 12 days, mail 13 days `[CALC]`.

**Statement of Information (SI-550)** `[M]`
- $25, "Due within 90 days of initial registration and every year thereafter."

- Late penalty: Corp. Code §2204 refers to the penalty in R&TC §19141 `[S-mirror]`. The amount was not opened `[U]`.

## 4. Tax and recurring obligations

**FTB, corporations** `[M]` ([FTB corporations](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/corporations/index.html), last updated 12/16/2025)
- "Every corporation that is incorporated, registered, or doing business in California must pay the $800 minimum franchise tax."
- "On or after January 1, 2020, newly incorporated or qualified corporations are not required to pay the minimum franchise tax in their first taxable year."
- The minimum tax also does not apply if no business was done in California and the tax year was 15 days or fewer.

**FTB, S corporations** `[M]` ([FTB S corporations](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/corporations/s-corporations.html), last updated 05/28/2026)
- "We tax every S corporation that has California source income 1.5%."
- "Your minimum franchise tax ($800) is due the first quarter of each accounting period."
- "We waive the minimum tax on newly formed or qualified S corporations filing an initial return for their first taxable year."
- "Any first-year net income is still subject to the 1.5% tax rate."
- The return is due on the 15th day of the 3rd month after year end.

**Contrast: LLCs** `[M]` ([FTB LLC](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html), last updated 03/05/2026): the first-year $800 exemption applied only to tax years from Jan 1 2021 to before Jan 1 2024.

## 7. Who may prepare and file: Legal Document Assistants (B&P §6400 et seq.) `[S-mirror]`

**Definitions (§6400)**
- A "Legal document assistant" (LDA) is any non-exempt person who, "for compensation," provides "any self-help service to a member of the public who is representing themselves in a legal matter" (§6400(c)(1)).
- The definition also covers "A corporation, partnership, association, or other entity that employs or contracts with any person not exempted" who does so (§6400(c)(2)).
- "Self-help service" (§6400(d)) means:
  1. "Completing legal documents in a ministerial manner, selected by a person who is representing themselves in a legal matter, by typing or otherwise completing the documents at the person's specific direction."
  2. Providing attorney-written or attorney-approved general published factual information. This, "in and of itself, does not require registration."
  3. Making published legal documents available.
  4. "Filing and serving legal forms and documents at the specific direction of a person who is representing themselves."
- "Legal matter" is not defined in the sections read `[U]`.

**What an LDA may not do**
- §6400(g): "may not provide any kind of advice, explanation, opinion, or recommendation to a consumer about possible legal rights, remedies, defenses, options, selection of forms, or strategies."
- §6401.6: an LDA must not serve a client who needs more than self-help service, and must tell the client to see an attorney.
- §6411: no false or misleading statements; no guarantees unless in writing with a factual basis; no claims of "special influence" with an agency; no unauthorized practice of law (UPL).
- §6401.5: registration "does not immunize any person from prosecution or liability pursuant to Section 6125, 6126, 6126.5, or 6127."

**Exemptions (§6401):** State Bar licensees and their staff, government employees, some nonprofits, persons providing "services that are regulated by federal law", and others.

**Registration, bond, county**
- **Where to register (§6402):** "registered … by the county clerk in the county in which their principal place of business is located, and in which they maintain a branch office". Disbarred or suspended attorneys may not register.
- **Eligibility (§6402.1):** one of:
  - High school diploma plus 2 years of law-related experience under an attorney.
  - Bachelor's degree plus 1 year of such experience.
  - Completion of a 24-unit paralegal program.
  - Completion of an ABA-approved paralegal program.
- **Continuing education (§6402.2):** 15 hours of CLE per two-year renewal.
- **Fee (§6404):** $175 for initial registration or renewal; $10 per extra ID card.
- **Bond (§6405):**
  - Individual: $25,000.
  - Partnership or corporation: $25,000 for 1–4 assistants, $50,000 for 5–9, $100,000 for 10 or more.
  - A cash deposit is allowed instead. The bond is recorded with the county recorder; $7 fee to file, cancel, revoke or withdraw it.
- **Term (§6406):** 2 years, or until the bond expires.
- **ID card (§6407):** must read "This person is not a lawyer." and "The county clerk has not evaluated this person's knowledge, experience, or services."
- **Penalties:**
  - §6415: violations of §§6401.6, 6402, 6408, 6410, 6411(a)–(c) or 6412.5 are misdemeanors, with a fine of $1,000–$2,000 per client, up to one year in jail, or both.
  - §6126(a): UPL is a misdemeanor, up to 1 year and/or a $1,000 fine.
- **Client contract (§6410):** see §8.
- **Venue (§6409.1):** the client's county of primary residence.
- **Client documents (§6409):** an LDA may not keep a client's original documents.

**Out-of-state or online providers**
- No section read addresses an LDA whose principal place of business is outside California. §6402 ties registration to the county of the principal place of business and of any branch office `[S-mirror]`. Whether an out-of-state online company serving California customers must register is not stated in the text read `[U]`.
- *Birbrower, Montalbano, Condon & Frank v. Superior Court* (1998) 17 Cal.4th 119, 128–129 `[M]` ([SCOCAL](https://scocal.stanford.edu/opinion/birbrower-montalbano-condon-frank-vsuperior-court-31892)), on UPL (§6125) by persons outside California:
  - "one may practice law in the state in violation of section 6125 although not physically present here by advising a California client on California law in connection with a California legal dispute by telephone, fax, computer, or other modern technological means."
  - The court rejects "the notion that a person automatically practices law 'in California' whenever that person … 'virtually' enters the state by telephone, fax, e-mail, or satellite."
- *In re Reynoso*, 477 F.3d 1117 (9th Cir. 2007) `[M]` ([opinion](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/477/477.F3d.1117.04-17190.html)):
  - Applying California law, the court held that a seller of web-based bankruptcy-petition software (Frankfort Digital Services / Ziinet) "engaged in the unauthorized practice of law".
  - Grounds: it held itself out as offering legal expertise, and its software selected exemptions the debtor did not choose.
  - The court cites *People v. Landlords Professional Services* (1989) 215 Cal.App.3d 1599: "merely clerical preparation services do not constitute the practice of law".
- No State Bar opinion or court decision was found that applies §6400 et seq. to Secretary of State business-entity filings `[U]`.
- A search found no LDA registration for LegalZoom or any other national filer `[U]`.

## 8. Advertising rules for document-preparation services `[S-mirror]`

- **§6408 (LDAs):**
  - The registrant's "name, business address, telephone number, registration number, and county of registration shall appear in any solicitation or advertisement, and on any printed papers or documents".
  - These details plus the registration expiration date must also appear "on any Internet Web site maintained by the registrant, and in any solicitation, advertisement, document, or correspondence … in electronic form."
- **§6411(f):** an LDA may not use "legal aid," "legal services," or similar terms in its business name or advertising that could mislead people about nonprofit or government status.
- **§6410(b) (LDA client contract):** must include the services, the costs, and the county clerk's contact information. It must also carry these statements in 12-point bold:
  - The LDA "is not an attorney and may not perform the legal services that an attorney performs".
  - The county clerk "has not evaluated or approved the registrant's knowledge or experience".
  - Free or low-cost help is available through bar associations or legal aid, and where to report fraud or UPL.
  - The LDA "is not permitted to engage in the practice of law".
- **§6410, other contract rules:**
  - The contract must be written in the language used in the sales presentation (§6410(c)).
  - The client may rescind within 24 hours (§6410(f)).
- **§6412.5:** no waiver of the chapter.
- **§17533.6 (any nongovernmental entity, including private filing services):** content that "reasonably could be interpreted … as implying any federal, state, or local government … connection, approval, or endorsement" is unlawful unless the solicitation meets subdivision (c). Those conditions include:
  - The front and back of every page must carry "THIS PRODUCT OR SERVICE HAS NOT BEEN APPROVED OR ENDORSED BY ANY GOVERNMENTAL AGENCY, AND THIS OFFER IS NOT BEING MADE BY AN AGENCY OF THE GOVERNMENT."
  - A mailed envelope must carry "THIS IS NOT A GOVERNMENT DOCUMENT."
- Google Ads government-documents policy: see round 1 ([03](03-multistate-professional-entity-market.md) §8). Not re-researched.
