### 3b. Proposed Security Rule changes

- Notice: "HIPAA Security Rule To Strengthen the Cybersecurity of Electronic Protected Health Information," 90 FR 898, Jan 6 2025, FR Doc 2024-30983, RIN 0945-AA22, 125 pages, comments closed Mar 7 2025 `[M]` text from https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2025/01/06/2024-30983.txt and https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-01-06/pdf/2024-30983.pdf (API metadata: federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2024-30983.json).
- New or changed required items, taken from the NPRM text I downloaded `[M]`:
  - Written technology asset inventory and network map, reviewed at least every 12 months.
  - Written risk analysis with specified content; reviewed at least every 12 months.
  - Multi-factor authentication, with stated exceptions (the NPRM discusses a solo practitioner with a practice-management system lacking MFA, and says the timeframe "may be different" for a solo practitioner than for a 5,000-user system).
  - Encryption of ePHI at rest and in transit, with exceptions.
  - Automated vulnerability scans at least every 6 months or per risk analysis, whichever is more frequent (proposed 45 CFR 164.312(h)(2)(i)(A)).
  - Penetration testing by "a qualified person" at least every 12 months or per risk analysis, whichever is more frequent (164.312(h)(2)(iii)).
  - Written procedures to restore critical systems and data within 72 hours; backups; incident response plan.
  - Network segmentation; anti-malware; patch management program.
  - Annual Security Rule compliance audit (HHS paperwork burden: 2 hours per regulated entity).
  - Business associates to verify technical safeguards to covered entities in writing at least every 12 months; business associates to notify covered entities within 24 hours of activating a contingency plan; covered entities to notify others within 24 hours of a workforce member's access change.
  - Removal of the "addressable" versus "required" distinction (NPRM's main structural change).
- Timeline stated: effective date 60 days after publication of a final rule; compliance date 180 days after the effective date (45 CFR 160.105); HHS text: "the total time frame for compliance from date of publication of a final rule would be 240 days" `[M]`.
- HHS cost estimate `[M]`: first-year cost about $9 billion; years two to five about $6 billion a year; annualized $6.8 billion at 2 percent (Table 1 shows $34 billion undiscounted, $32 billion at 2 percent, $7 billion annualized, 2026 to 2030). Regulatory Flexibility analysis: 740,348 small entities affected; "annualized costs per regulated entity of approximately $1,235 [= $2.3 billion / 1,822,600 regulated entities]", per establishment, discounted 2 percent, and "only small firms whose revenues are below $41,167 [= $1,235 / 0.03]" would see an effect over 3 percent. HHS also says "the cost of compliance" would not "fall disproportionately on small entities." No separate first-year dollar figure for a solo practice was found in the text `[U]`.
- Status: Unified Agenda RIN 0945-AA22, Final Action 07/00/2027, stage Long-Term Actions `[M]` per round-1 file 05 (reginfo.gov pubId 202510). Davis Wright Tremaine (July 2026): HHS moved the target from May 2026 to July 2027; status changed from "final rule stage" to "long term actions"; the rule is "no longer on the 2026 Agency Rule List"; the authors expect HHS to finalize only some proposals `[S]` https://www.dwt.com/blogs/privacy--security-law-blog/2026/07/hhs-updates-hipaa-rulemaking-timeframes . The same piece says the April 4 2026 OMB submission applies to the separate Privacy Rule coordinated-care rule (RIN 0945-AA00), not the Security Rule, and a right-of-access proposed rule (RIN 0945-AA28) is expected Nov 2026 `[S]`. No OCR press statement about the delay was found `[U]`. No final rule in the Federal Register as of Nov 18 2026 `[S]`.
