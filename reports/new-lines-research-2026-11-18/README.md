# New lines of business: fact-gathering brief (Nov 18 2026)

Owner: Sid. Decision maker for this research: the parent agent. **Research agents gather facts only.**

## Who is asking (context only)

A New York filing service for licensed professionals. It forms NY PLLCs end to end (state education-department approval, Department of State filing, newspaper publication, EIN), about 600 formations a year, about 75% healthcare (therapists, psychiatric NPs, physicians, dentists). It also sells registered agent service, a virtual mail address, and (new) insurance credentialing. One owner; most paperwork is prepared by AI agents, so form-filling costs almost nothing. The question is which **new offerings** exist. Do not put the business's name, the owner's name or any client detail in a web query.

## Rules for every research agent (hard)

1. **No judgment.** Do not recommend, rank, score, or say an idea is good, bad, attractive, risky or a fit. No "should". No verdicts. If you catch yourself concluding, delete it and report the underlying fact.
2. **No estimates of your own.** Report source figures. If you add or multiply source figures, show the arithmetic and tag it `[CALC]`. Never build a market size from assumed percentages.
3. **Tag every figure and claim:** `[M]` you opened the primary source yourself (agency, statute, the company's own page, a filing dataset) · `[S]` secondary (trade press, vendor blog, review site, search snippet) · `[U]` looked for, could not find or could not open. Give the URL and the date of the data next to each. Prefer 2025–2026 sources. Today is Nov 18 2026.
4. **Primary sources first.** For legal requirements quote the statute or agency page and cite the section. For prices, read the provider's own pricing page. A search snippet alone is `[S]`.
5. **Read-only.** Do not sign up, submit forms, email, call, post, buy, or contact anyone. Do not edit any file except your own report file (and helper scripts in this folder's `scripts/`). Never run a command with a live flag (`--confirm`, `--live`, `--send`, `--submit`).
6. **Say what you did not find.** End with a "Not found / not verified" list.
7. **Already researched or decided. Do not research these:** insurance credentialing, payer enrollment, CAQH, credentialing upkeep and group retainers; medical billing / claims; MSO + management-LLC pairs; NY domestic PC formation; a NY personal-license filing service (NYSED); the NY Board of Pharmacy out-of-state agent; registered agent; virtual mail; biennial-statement compliance plan; DBA, amendment, good-standing, certified-copy and dissolution filings in NY; S-corp election; NY sales tax certificate; foreign qualification into NY; plain (non-professional) LLC formation; publication-only services; Mercury / Gusto style affiliate links; buying another company; collaborating-physician matching.

## What to report for each service line you are assigned

Use this order, one section per line:

1. **What it is and who buys it.** The legal requirement or event that creates the demand, with citation.
2. **Annual volume of the triggering event** (filings, applications, registrants, renewals), US and New York where available, from primary data.
3. **Who sells it today:** 5–8 named providers with their published prices (one-time and recurring), from their own pages. Note what each price includes.
4. **Size evidence for those providers:** customer counts, revenue, funding, employee counts, businesses-for-sale listings. Source each.
5. **What it legally takes to offer it:** licenses, registrations, bonding, insurance, unauthorized-practice-of-law or professional-title limits, with citations. Federal and New York.
6. **The work itself:** each step, whether it is an online portal or form, paper, wet ink, notary, phone or in person; government fees; published processing times.
7. **Recurrence:** renewals or annual obligations, their frequency and fees.
8. **Advertising limits stated in platform policy** for the category (for example Google Ads "government documents and official services").
9. **Public search-demand figures**, if any, with source.
10. **Not found / not verified.**
