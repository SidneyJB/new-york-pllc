# Round 1 read: which new lines go to round 2 (Nov 18 2026)

**Written by:** the parent agent. These are its judgments on the eleven fact reports in this folder. **Status: working view for Sid, not a decision.** Nothing here has been sent, built or priced.

**Limits of round 1.** The agents shared one web-search budget and it ran out, so seven of the nine outside-market reports have gaps (listed at the end of each). The state-by-state entity rules report (`02`) is mostly blank. No line has a measured cost to win a customer.

**What our own customers ask for** ([01](01-internal-demand-signals.md)): very little. Over 12 months, 506 clients sent 4,552 emails. The most-requested thing we don't sell was forming or registering in another state (5 of 58 "do you also offer" messages). Taxes: 22 clients mentioned them, none of 15 checked asked us to do them. So no line here has the unprompted pull credentialing had; none can assume easy cross-sell.

## Not going further, and why

| Line | Reason |
|---|---|
| NY LLC Transparency Act filings | The state says only LLCs formed in a foreign country are covered (43 found). No market. |
| Multi-state entity setups for telehealth companies | The $10–15K prices include supplying a physician owner, which we can't. Sold to companies by cold outreach, where our record is 1 reply in 38. |
| Liquor licences, NYC consumer licences, MWBE, cannabis, food permits ([09](09-ny-license-permit-expediting.md)) | Liquor has real volume (6,322 licences and 35,704 permits a year), but the work includes community-board notices, hearings and site documents, the buyer is one we have never sold to, and no provider price could be verified. The others have low fees or small counts. |
| SAM.gov, trucking authority, FDA agent ([10](10-federal-high-volume-filings.md)) | Big volumes, but the sellers charge for free government registrations and have drawn FTC and watchdog action. Ad platforms now gate the category. No link to our customers. |
| Trademarks | Only attorneys may represent applicants (37 CFR 11.14). Blocked. |
| Malpractice insurance | Therapist premiums are about $150 a year. A referral fee can't depend on a sale. Physician premiums are large, but selling needs a licensed person and carrier appointments, and no commission rate could be verified. |
| Practice websites, HIPAA email, software referrals ([06](06-practice-launch-services-insurance.md)) | Possible add-ons, not lines: $100–200 one-time referral fees, thin reseller margins, and websites compete with $19 builders and the site bundled into practice software. |
| DEA, CLIA, first-hire setup | Small, infrequent, and no provider was found charging for DEA or CLIA help. Could be reminders inside D. |
| Apostilles | One-time, $50–495, needs someone at a state counter. |
