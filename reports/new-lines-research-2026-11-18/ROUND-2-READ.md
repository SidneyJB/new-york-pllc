# New lines of business: final read after two rounds (Nov 18 2026)

**Written by:** the parent agent, from the 16 fact reports in this folder. **Status: its judgment, for Sid to decide on.** Nothing has been sent, built, priced or tested. The earlier cut is in [ROUND-1-READ.md](ROUND-1-READ.md).

## Bottom line

**The research did not find a new line with both very high earnings and a very high chance of success.** It found three moderate ones, each worth roughly $100–250K a year by 2028 if it works, each at well under even odds on today's evidence, and each testable for under about $2,000. Odds below are the parent agent's judgment, not measurements. No line has a measured cost to win a customer, and our own customers have barely asked for any of them ([01](01-internal-demand-signals.md)).

## Add-ons, not lines

- **State licence applications for clinicians** ([14](14-clinician-licensing-round2.md)): $249–500 per state, one-time, with many steps only the clinician can do. Worth offering on the credentialing site; about $35K a year at 100 licences.
- **HIPAA compliance documents** ([15](15-hipaa-compliance-round2.md)): the market leaders have about 3,000 customers each at $99–115 a month; enforcement against solo practices is rare (3–12 named small cases a year nationally, none by the NY AG under 10 clinicians); a free federal tool exists; search volume is 590 a month at $37 a click. At most a small extra inside credentialing upkeep. Revisit if the proposed Security Rule is finalized (listed for July 2027).
