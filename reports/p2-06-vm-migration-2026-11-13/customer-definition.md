# P2-06 - Initial customer definition (existing-practice VM migration)

Draft, Nov 13 2026. Agent. Needs Sid sign-off.

## Who the pilot is for

An **existing New York PLLC (or PC)** that:

1. Is already formed and active with NY DOS. Not a new formation. New formations stay on the P2-05 checkout / OP-approved path.
2. Has a **home address** as its public DOS address today (the "address for service of process" line). This is the clearest mail and privacy problem we solve.
3. Has a **real business-mail need**: state and tax notices, payer and licensing mail, legal papers, bank mail. It is not only looking for a "nicer address".
4. Will use our address **only for mail and the public DOS record**, and keeps a real practice location (or a home-office / telehealth arrangement it already reports) for clinical and payer purposes.
5. Has an owner who can sign and notarize USPS Form 1583 and supply two IDs before we activate.
6. Has a forwarding address in the US for weekly mail.
7. Was a past customer we may contact, or was introduced by a partner who asked us to help.

The best first fit is a **telehealth or office-light practice** (psychiatric NP, LCSW, LMHC, LMFT, psychology) whose owner's home is on the DOS record. These owners rarely need our address for anything clinical.

## Exclusions for the pilot

Say no, or quote separately, when any of these apply:

| Exclusion | Why |
|---|---|
| Wants our address as the **practice location** for NPPES, payer enrollment, CAQH, a DEA registration, a pharmacy, or patient visits | A mailing address is not a clinical location. Our own FAQ says many payers, NPPES and banks need a physical practice location. |
| Any **unresolved permitted-use question** (for example: "can I list this with Medicare?", "can patients send records here?" in volume, controlled-substance shipments) | Not settled for the base service (P1-05 permitted-use bullet is still open). |
| **Foreign** entity (formed outside NY, qualified here) | Different form (foreign Certificate of Change) and home-state address rules. Quote separately later. |
| **Law firms** | Bar and court address rules are different. Hold until Sid decides. |
| **Non-professional LLCs** | Not a professional entity. Can buy VM, but not part of this pilot test. |
| Entity **not in good standing** with DOS, or with an unfinished formation step (publication not done, overdue biennial statement) | Fix that first, quoted separately. |
| Customer wants a **name change, amendment, or member change** at the same time | Different filing (Certificate of Amendment, NYSED consent). Quote separately. |
| Customer needs **daily mail, scanning, or same-day pickup** | We forward weekly, on Fridays. Scanning is not in the agreement. |
| High package volume, medical samples, drugs, or other prohibited or hazardous items | Agreement 6.2. |
| Already a VM client, or cancelled VM in the past | Not a migration. Former VM clients are a win-back question, not this pilot. |
| Formed under 90 days ago | Handled by the P2-05 attach path. |
| Any contact on a do-not-contact or blocklist, or who asked not to be emailed | Permission. |
| Partner-owned customers (white-label or enterprise accounts) | The partner owns the relationship. Only take them through a partner introduction. |

## What we will not promise

- That our address is acceptable for any payer, board, bank, NPPES, or DEA use.
- That moving the DOS address removes the owner's home address from every public record. Older filings stay on the DOS record. NYSED, the IRS, and banks keep their own addresses.
- A unique suite number until the PMB numbering in the CRM is live (see scope.md, Form 1583 section).
