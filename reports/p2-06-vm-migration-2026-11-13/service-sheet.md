# Move your PLLC's public address to our business address

## Who this is for

Your New York PLLC is already formed. Your home address is on the public New York Department of State record. You want a business address there instead, and someone to receive and forward your entity's mail.

## What you get

- **A business mailing address** at 1 Blue Hill Plaza, Pearl River, NY 10965, for your entity's mail and the public state record.
- **Registered agent service**, included.
- **Weekly forwarding.** Mail is forwarded every Friday to the US address you choose.
- **Signature for certified and registered mail**, and packages accepted.
- **Junk mail is not forwarded** unless you ask in writing.

## What we do to move you

1. Prepare your USPS Form 1583 (required by postal rules for mail received through an agent).
2. File a **Certificate of Change** with the NY Department of State to put our address on your public record.
3. Prepare a short letter for you to sign so **NYSED** has your entity's new address. The state filing does not update NYSED.
4. Set up forwarding and tell you when your first mail goes out.

## What we need from you

- Your acceptance of our [Mail Forwarding & Registered Agent Service Agreement](https://www.nypllc.com/mail-forwarding-agreement).
- A signed and **notarized** USPS Form 1583, with two IDs: a government photo ID and a separate proof of address. Online notary works.
- Your forwarding address.
- Your signature on the NYSED letter.

We file with the state only after we have your Form 1583. That way your mail is never sent to an address we are not yet ready to handle.

## Timing: three different dates

1. **Service start.** When your paperwork is complete. Billing starts here.
2. **Filing done.** The state usually updates the public record within a few business days of filing. NYSED updates later and does not send a confirmation.
3. **Closing your old arrangement.** Wait until the public record shows our address and you have updated the senders that matter to you. We suggest at least 30 days.

## What this service is not

- **Not a practice location.** Do not list our address as the place you see patients or clients. Many payers, NPPES, DEA and banks need a physical practice address.
- **Not daily mail or scanning.** We forward weekly.
- **Not a full privacy service.** Earlier state filings stay public. NYSED, the IRS and your bank keep their own records. You update the IRS (Form 8822-B), banks, payers and your own license address yourself.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in writing. Because our address is on your public record, cancellation is complete once the state record shows a new address. You can file that change yourself and send us proof, or we file it for $129. Full terms are in the agreement.
