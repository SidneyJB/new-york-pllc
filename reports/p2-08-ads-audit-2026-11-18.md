| Sales-Search-1 week | Impr | IS | Rank-lost | Budget-lost | Eligible (impr ÷ IS) | Avg CPC | Conv (click date) | CVR |

| Oct 19 | 789 | 22.0% | 78.0% | 0% | 3,581 | $12.17 | 8 | 15% |

- **The eligible pool did not shrink.** It grew from 3.6k to 4.4k a week. So this is not a negative-keyword or self-block problem like the Aug 4 incident, and it is not lost demand.
- **No changes in the account:** the only change event since Oct 20 is the Oct 21 API unpause of `816286133015`. The portfolio tCPA is still $105. Budget-lost is 0%.
- **The mechanism is bid-down under Target CPA.** CVR by click date fell from 20% to 4% at a fixed $105 target, and avg CPC fell from about $12 to $9.87 in the week of Nov 9. Lower bids lose more auctions on rank, so IS falls.
- Part of that CVR fall is **lag**: by conversion date the week of Nov 9 has 5 purchases, not 2, and the click-date figure will keep rising.
- Part is real: CRM Google orders went 11 → 9 → 7 → 6 a week.
- Policy limitation is not the driver. The Sales RSA has been APPROVED_LIMITED since July, so it is a constant.
- `01` IS actually rose, from about 19% to 25–30%, after the Oct 21 unpause. `03` is flat at 35–45%.
- Auction Insights (competitor pressure in November) is UI-only; Sid can export it, but it is not needed for this audit.
