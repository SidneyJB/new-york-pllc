# PC pilot costing and test price

Conventions follow the [decision log](../../decision-log.md), Nov 9 row: **labor is not costed in dollars.** Minutes are tracked for capacity only. Contribution = price − government and third-party cash − Stripe (3% + $0.30). PLLC benchmark: **$514 contribution at $885** (P1-03, via the [P2-11 model](../p2-11-model-2026-11-11/README.md)). The CRM PLLC COGS constants are $345 (articles $235 incl. expedite and certified copy, NYSED $10, legal notices $50, cert pub $50).

The P1-03 folder `../evidence/p1-03-unit-economics-2026-11-13/README.md/` was not on disk in this checkout, so the $514 figure is taken from the P2-11 README and the decision log.

At 5 pilot cases that is about 9 extra hours spread over roughly 3 months, within Sid's capacity. Fixing the CRM gaps (generator, $90 check, workflow step, completion email) would cut about 60 minutes, which brings a PC near PLLC effort. **Elapsed time:** NYSED consent took 58 days in Case A (submit to consent). DOS is 1–2 days with expedite. With no publication, a PC should finish in **~75–100 days** if the certified copy goes in on time, vs a 117-day PLLC median. Case A took 124 days because the corporate records and the certified copy came late.

## 4. Recommendation: test **$985** for a standard PC; keep **$1,285** for MSO-ready

- At $885 the steady-state contribution beats the PLLC. During the pilot, though, the template review and roughly 110 extra minutes mean parity earns less than a PLLC per case ($378 vs $514). A PC at $885 would subsidize a lower-demand, more manual product.
- **$985** brings pilot-period contribution roughly level with a PLLC ($475 vs $514) and lands at **$675** once the templates are paid for. The $100 premium matches real extra work (corporate records, the second NYSED filing), not a publication saving, so it fits the Sep 3 "no publication-skipping discount" rule.
- $1,085 earns more, but there are only ~4 domestic asks a year, two of them price-anchored at $785–$885 by our own earlier quotes. Losing one buyer to price would cost more than the extra $97 per case gains. Revisit $1,085 after 3 paid cases if none push back.
- **MSO-ready $1,285 stands** (contribution $891 with a kit). Its fee list changes only by dropping the repealed $10 organization tax, so the price and the customer's total are unchanged.
- This is a pilot price for manual quotes. It does not change the $885 PLLC hold (Nov 9) or the site.
