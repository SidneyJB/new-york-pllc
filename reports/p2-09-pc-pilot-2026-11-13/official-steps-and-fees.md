# Domestic NY PC: official steps and fees (verified Nov 13 2026)

Not legal advice. "Verified" means we read it on the official page or in the statute text on Nov 13 2026. NYSED's own site (op.nysed.gov) would not load in our fetch tool because of a certificate error, so NYSED figures come from search-indexed copies of NYSED pages and PDFs. The NYSED fee chart PDF is marked Rev. 5/23. **Before the first pilot filing, Ops should open the live NYSED pages in a browser and confirm the $90 / $20 / $105 figures.**

## Our current CRM vs these steps (build gaps; drafts only, nothing changed)

1. **NYSED check amount.** The domestic package builder pays **$10 x owners** for every domestic entity (`crm/lib/check-template/build-check-sheet-rows-for-pllc.ts` calls `pllc-application-check-amount-from-members.ts`). A domestic PC needs **$90**. This is the same mistake that got the Fagan foreign PC package returned in August.
2. **Wrong DOS form.** `generateArticlesForPllc` fills the PLLC **Articles of Organization** for any non-LLC, so PCs get it too. A PC needs a BCL Certificate of Incorporation that includes a share clause, the professional purpose and the officers/directors. The auto-filing worker's domestic path is "professional LLC". **PCs must be filed by hand.** The articles generator also falls back to Rockland county; a PC should use the actual office county, since there is no publication reason to pick a county.
3. **Operating agreement generated for a PC** (Case A). PCs need bylaws, a consent, stock certificates and a ledger instead.
4. **`ny-pc` workflow** (`crm/lib/workflows/definitions/ny.ts`) has no step for the NYSED certified-copy filing with its 30-day clock. It still carries the PLLC `CERT_OF_PUBLICATION_CORRECTION_NEEDED` step and a "PLLC name" label.
5. **No domestic-PC intake path.** Foreign PCs use the `foreign-pc-manual` manual order path; Case A came in through the PLLC checkout with `Order.entityType = PLLC`.
6. **Formation-complete email and FAQ PDF** are PLLC-only (publication, Rockland). PCs need their own short completion email.
