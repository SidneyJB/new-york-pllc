# PC pilot scope (draft for Sid)

**What this is:** preparation for P3-05, up to 5 paid domestic NY PC cases. Each case is quoted and invoiced by hand. Cases come from partners and inbound asks, not from the site or ads. Per the [Nov 8 revised priorities](../../ops/revised-priorities-2026-11-08.md), nothing launches until a partner surfaces a live case.

## Route to counsel (we stop and refer; no drafting, no opinion)

- Any owner, director or officer who is not licensed in the PC's profession (spouse, investor, manager, MSO, DE C Corp, trust).
- MSO / MSA structures beyond forming the practice PC itself. Independent counsel drafts the MSA ([mso-msa.md](../../memory-bank/features/mso-msa.md)). We file only the PC under the MSO-ready constraints.
- Shareholders' agreements, buy-sell, vesting, unequal or multiple share classes, preferred stock.
- Converting or merging an existing PLLC or PC, or a PC owning another entity (as in demand case B's partnership-agreement issue).
- Multi-profession PCs; whether a service is a "profession" at all (demand case D, a healthcare navigation advisory).
- Corporate-practice, fee-splitting, Article 28, payer or Medicare/Medicaid questions.
- "PC or PLLC?" tax or liability advice. The customer decides with their own adviser (P3-05 first bullet). We give only the factual differences: publication, corporate records, triennial statement.

## Explicitly out

Law (Appellate Division route), engineering, land surveying, geology, architecture, landscape architecture (D.P.C. and certificate of authorization), CPA firms, veterinary until asked, foreign PCs (already a separate published product), DE C Corp formation, anything outside NY, year-2+ compliance filings (the calendar only), payroll and tax registrations.

## Entity-choice disclosure (one paragraph in every quote)

> We form either a PLLC or a professional corporation. We do not advise which one is right for your taxes or liability. Please confirm the choice with your accountant or attorney. The main practical differences: a PC has no newspaper publication requirement; a PC keeps corporate records (bylaws, stock certificates) and files a statement with the Education Department every three years; NYSED charges a PC more at formation.
